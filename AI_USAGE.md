# AI usage

Project: "Neighborhood EXAMM" proposal, DSCI.650
Tool: Claude (Anthropic), through the Claude Code desktop app

## Proposal

- **Checking against the criteria.** I used AI to review my first draft against the assignment's grading criteria and requirements.
- **Planning revisions.** After that review, I used it to write notes on what was missing. It gave me a structure of sections and placeholders, and I filled in the content myself.
- **Literature.** I used it to search for additional literature on the topic, to help with parts of the literature review, and to check whether my ideas had already been implemented.
- **Language.** I brainstormed the ideas myself. I used AI to check whether the text is coherent in English.

I added all references to the bibliography myself. AI output is not cited as a source.
